standuser

Drop your Letterboxd export. Get a JoJo-style Stand card: six stats graded against 34,523 Letterboxd users, on a table of 9,787 films.

The link contains your username and grades, and the card's text.

Drop letterboxd-…-utc.zip here

or

Get it at Letterboxd, Settings, Data, Export your data. The ZIP is read in this tab and never uploaded; the page makes no requests after it loads.

Only watched.csv, ratings.csv and diary.csv are read, plus two columns of profile.csv (Date Joined and Favorite Films). Nothing else in profile.csv is read.

Advanced

Only needed to override the file name. The join date is read from the Date Joined column of profile.csv; without that file or column it is the earliest date in watched.csv.

Why these grades

Each stat is graded against 34,523 Letterboxd users. Films that are not in the built-in film table are skipped by every stat that needs film data.
